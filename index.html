Here's a bit more about me

Frontend Software Engineer

I'm Christi, a Frontend Software Engineer with a laser focus on how users experience what I build. Twenty years running retail operations taught me to notice the small stuff that makes or breaks someone's experience — I bring that same obsessive attention to detail into every pixel and every line of code. I don't quit until it's right, and "it's basically working" has never been good enough for me. If there's a bug, I'll find it. If there's a rough edge, I'll smooth it. That same relentless drive now powers how I build.
